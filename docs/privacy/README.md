# Privacy and GDPR

TackWise follows privacy-by-design and privacy-by-default.

GPS/location and race telemetry linked or linkable to a TackWise user are treated as personal data.

Working documents:
- `data-inventory.md`
- `gdpr-processing-record.md`
- `retention-policy.md`
- `privacy-design.md`

These are living engineering/compliance documents and are not a substitute for final legal review of public privacy notices or terms.
