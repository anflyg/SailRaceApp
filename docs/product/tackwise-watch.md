# TackWise Watch

## Role

TackWise Watch is a sailing-focused watch/companion device for instantly readable race information with minimal interaction.

It must not attempt to reproduce the complete iPhone interface.

## Design goals

- Excellent sunlight readability.
- Low power consumption.
- Large digits and strong contrast.
- Robust operation in wet and unstable conditions.
- Minimal menu depth.
- Physical-button-friendly interaction where hardware allows.

## Standalone operation

A key goal is useful standalone operation without an active phone connection. Candidate standalone functions include:
- countdown/start timer,
- GPS speed,
- heading/course,
- race timer,
- basic race recording.

Final capability depends on the selected hardware platform.

## Connected operation

When connected to TackWise Race, the watch can present selected high-value information calculated or managed by the phone, for example:
- countdown,
- speed,
- VMG,
- course/heading,
- distance/time to start line,
- mark or layline information.

## Interaction principles

Primary actions should require very little attention. Short press may perform the normal action; long press may be used for deliberate reset/stop operations where appropriate. Exact behaviour is a product decision and should be documented before implementation.
